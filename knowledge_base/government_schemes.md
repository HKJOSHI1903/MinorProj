# Government schemes for farmers

Source to verify against: pmkisan.gov.in, pmfby.gov.in, your bank, Common Service Centre or agriculture office. Amounts, rates, deadlines and eligibility rules change; confirm the current details before applying.

## PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

PM-KISAN is an income support scheme of the Government of India. Eligible landholding farmer families receive 6,000 rupees a year, paid directly into the bank account in three instalments of 2,000 rupees. Registration needs Aadhaar, land records and a bank account linked to Aadhaar. e-KYC must be completed to keep receiving instalments. You can register and check your payment status on the PM-KISAN portal, through the PM-KISAN mobile app, or at a Common Service Centre.

## Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY, crop insurance)

PMFBY insures notified crops against loss from natural causes such as drought, flood, hailstorm, cyclone, pests and diseases. The farmer pays a small share of the premium: up to 2 percent of the sum insured for kharif food and oilseed crops, 1.5 percent for rabi food and oilseed crops, and 5 percent for annual commercial and horticultural crops. The government pays the rest. Enrol before the cut-off date for the season through your bank, a Common Service Centre, an insurance agent or the crop insurance portal or app.

## Reporting a crop loss under PMFBY

For localised damage such as hailstorm, landslide or inundation, and for damage to harvested crop lying in the field, inform the insurance company, your bank or the agriculture office within 72 hours. You can report through the Crop Insurance app, the insurance company's toll-free number, or the bank or agriculture office. Keep photographs of the damage.

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

The Kisan Credit Card gives farmers short-term credit from banks for seed, fertiliser, pesticide and other cultivation costs, and also covers animal husbandry and fisheries. Crop loans up to 3 lakh rupees carry a subsidised interest rate, with a further reduction for farmers who repay on time. Apply at your bank branch, cooperative society or regional rural bank with identity proof, address proof and land records.

## Soil Health Card scheme

Under the Soil Health Card scheme, soil from your field is tested and you receive a card with fertiliser recommendations for your crops. Contact your agriculture office, soil testing laboratory or Krishi Vigyan Kendra to give a sample.

## e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)

e-NAM is an online trading platform that connects mandis across India so farmers can see prices and sell produce to buyers in other markets. Register at your nearest e-NAM mandi with identity proof and bank details.
