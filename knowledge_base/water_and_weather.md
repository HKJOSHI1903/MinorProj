# Water, irrigation and weather

Source to verify against: India Meteorological Department agromet advisories (Meghdoot app, mausam.imd.gov.in) and your Krishi Vigyan Kendra.

## When to irrigate

Irrigate at the stages when a shortage of water costs the most yield: for most crops these are establishment, flowering and grain or fruit filling. Check soil moisture by pressing a handful of soil from root depth; if it does not hold together, the crop needs water. Irrigate in the morning or evening to reduce evaporation.

## Saving water

Drip irrigation delivers water to the root zone and can save a large share of the water used in flood irrigation; it also keeps leaves dry, which reduces leaf diseases. Sprinklers suit closely sown crops. Mulching reduces evaporation. Level the field so water spreads evenly. Subsidy for drip and sprinkler systems is available under the Per Drop More Crop component of the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana; ask your agriculture or horticulture office.

## Too much water

Waterlogging starves roots of air and causes root rot and wilting. Make drainage channels before the monsoon and grow vegetables on raised beds in heavy rainfall areas.

## Weather and disease

Many fungal diseases spread when leaves stay wet for long periods. Several days of cloudy, humid or rainy weather are a warning to inspect the crop closely, especially potato and tomato for late blight. Hot, dry weather favours mites and sucking pests.

## Weather and spraying

Do not spray when rain is expected within a few hours, when the wind is strong, or in the midday heat. The best time is a calm early morning or late afternoon.

## Weather advisories

The India Meteorological Department issues district-level agromet advisories twice a week with a five-day forecast and crop advice. They are available on the Meghdoot mobile app and through Krishi Vigyan Kendras. The Damini app gives lightning warnings.

## Protecting crops from extreme weather

Before heavy rain, harvest mature produce, clear drainage channels and postpone fertiliser and pesticide application. Before a heat wave, give light, frequent irrigation and mulch the soil. Before frost, irrigate lightly in the evening, because moist soil holds more heat.
