# Where to get expert help

Source to verify against: kvk.icar.gov.in and the Department of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.

## Kisan Call Centre

The Kisan Call Centre answers farmers' questions on the telephone in local languages. Call the toll-free number 1800-180-1551 from any mobile or landline. Agriculture graduates answer questions on crops, pests, diseases, weather, schemes and market prices, and pass difficult questions to subject experts.

## Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK)

A Krishi Vigyan Kendra is a farm science centre run under the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR). Most districts have one. Its scientists identify crop diseases and pests from a plant sample, test soil and water, demonstrate new varieties and methods on farmers' fields, and run free training. Take an affected plant, including leaves, stem and roots, in a paper bag when you visit.

## When to ask an expert instead of relying on an app

Ask an expert when the app says its confidence is low, when the disease is spreading quickly through the field, when the first treatment has not worked after a week, when you are not sure which product or dose to use, and before using any product you have not used before. A photo-based app sees one leaf; an expert can examine the whole plant, the soil and the field.

## Agriculture department and extension officers

Your block or district agriculture office has agriculture officers and extension workers who advise on seeds, fertiliser, schemes and subsidies and who visit fields. They are also the contact for crop loss reports and scheme applications.

## What to tell the expert

Say which crop and variety you grow and how old the crop is, when you first saw the problem and how fast it is spreading, which part of the plant is affected, what the weather has been like, and what fertiliser and sprays you have already used. Photographs of the whole plant and of a close-up help.
